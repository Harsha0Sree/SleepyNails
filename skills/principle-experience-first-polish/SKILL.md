# Experience-First Polish

Newer models have significantly stronger design taste and ergonomic judgment than older ones. Use that strength without changing what the product does.

- Replace generic templates (pill badge clutter, nested cards inside cards, low-contrast text, uneven grids) with cohesive typography, disciplined spacing, and clear hierarchy.
- Ensure every interactive surface has accessible labels, visible keyboard focus rings, responsive mobile layouts, and complete empty, loading, and error states.
- Keep routine UI interactions snappy and free of layout thrashing.
